Paste a screenshot to start

Grab part of your screen with any screenshot tool, then press Ctrl + V here. You can also drop an image file onto this page.

Crop it, pixelate private details, record highlights and boxes, then press S to play the whole sequence for OBS.

Paper B-Roll

Drag the paper to reposition it. Scroll the wheel over it to resize.

Drag to draw a new crop, drag inside to move it, drag edges or corners to resize. Enter applies, Esc cancels.

Drag to add a pixelated area. Click one to select it, then drag to move or resize. Block size applies to every area.

Drag across the paper to record a stroke, or drag out a box with the Box style. The highlighter always draws a straight line; other strokes follow the mouse (hold Shift for straight). Each style keeps its own settings, shared by everything drawn in that style.
