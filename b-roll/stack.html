Images

    You can also drop images anywhere on the window or paste them with Ctrl/⌘+V. Top of the list enters first; use ▲ ▼ to reorder. Moving or resizing a card in Edit layout pins it, and Auto-layout keeps pinned cards where they are.

    In Edit layout: drag to move · corner handle or wheel to resize · top handle or Shift+wheel to rotate (Shift while dragging snaps 15°) · arrow keys nudge · Esc exits.

    Timing

    cubic-bezier(x1, y1, x2, y2), values 0–1. Curve shows how many cards have landed over time.

    Entrances

    Idle wiggle

    Each card eases into its own loop right after it lands and keeps going forever. Strength 0 turns it off. Paused in Edit layout so cards stay put while you arrange them.

    Sound

    Changing the sound plays a preview. Min spacing skips hits that land closer together than this, so the fast finale doesn't turn into a buzz.

    Look

    Background

    Size, tilt and margin re-arrange unpinned cards. For green-screen keying, shadows and motion blur leave soft edges; turn the shadow off if your keyer struggles.

    Frame

    Drag or swipe on the canvas to move the whole stack; in Edit layout, drag empty space. Scroll up or down to zoom in or out around the cursor; pinch works too.

    Recording

    Canvas is 1920×1080 and always scales to fit the window without cropping. In fullscreen on a 1080p display it maps 1:1.
