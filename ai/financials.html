1 Google cannibalized search

AI Overviews nearly halve clicks on regular results, which hurts organic links and ads alike. And each AI answer costs Google several times more to produce than the cheap keyword lookup its business was built on.

  • Google users are less likely to click on links when an AI summary appearsPrimary
    Pew Research Center · Jul 2025
    The core data: 8% of visits with an AI summary led to a click on a regular result, versus 15% without one, and only 1% clicked a link inside the summary.
  • For tech giants, AI like Bing and Bard poses billion-dollar search problem
    Reuters · Feb 2023
    Alphabet's chairman estimates an LLM exchange costs about 10x a standard keyword search.
  • Google AI Overviews are killing the web: Pew study
    The Register · Jul 2025
    Readable write-up of the Pew findings and what they mean for publishers.
  • Pew: Google AI Overviews lead to fewer clicksCounterpoint
    Search Engine Roundtable · Jul 2025
    Includes the study's charts and Google's objections to its methodology.

2 Cheaper tokens, pricier tasks

The price per token keeps falling, but newer models use far more tokens per task. OpenAI's inference costs quadrupled in 2025, it loses money on its $200 plan, and the startups building on these models pay more for AI than they earn.

  • Research paper on frontier model price trendsPrimary
    arXiv 2511.23455 · Nov 2025
    The price for a fixed level of performance falls 5–10x a year, while the cost of running frontier models rises 3–18x a year.
  • OpenAI expects $600 bn in compute spending through 2030 ahead of IPO
    Business Standard / Reuters, citing The Information · 2026
    Inference costs rose fourfold in 2025, and adjusted gross margin fell from 40% to 33%.
  • OpenAI is losing money on its pricey ChatGPT Pro plan, CEO Sam Altman says
    TechCrunch · Jan 2025
    Even at $200 a month, heavy users cost more to serve than they pay.
  • Have AI gross margins really turned the corner?Counterpoint
    SaaStr
    Cursor's estimated −30% gross margin, alongside evidence that the AI labs' own margins are improving.

3 95% free riders

ChatGPT has hundreds of millions of weekly users, but only about 1 in 20 pay for it. The rest cost money to serve, so the business plan depends on ads and commerce that don't exist at scale yet.

  • OpenAI's ChatGPT is so popular that almost no one will pay for it
    The Register · Oct 2025
    The 5% figure, attributed to a senior OpenAI executive via the FT, plus Menlo Ventures' estimate that only 3% of generative AI users pay.
  • ChatGPT user base surges 350% in 18 months as it nears 1 billion weekly active usersPrimary
    9to5Mac, quoting OpenAI · Feb 2026
    OpenAI's own update: 900M weekly users and more than 50M consumer subscribers, still just over 5%.
  • OpenAI projects 220 million paying ChatGPT users by 2030Counterpoint
    Reuters via The Star, citing The Information · Nov 2025
    OpenAI's plan to lift paid conversion to about 8.5% by 2030.

6 Circular money

Chipmakers invest in AI labs, and the labs spend that money on the same chipmakers' hardware, often through cloud providers that are also tied into the deals. Revenue moves around the circle, but little new money enters from outside it.

  • OpenAI, Nvidia Fuel $1 Trillion AI Market With Web of Circular DealsPaywall
    Bloomberg · Oct 2025
    The definitive map of who is paying whom.
  • AI's trillion dollar deal wheel bubbling around Nvidia, OpenAI
    The Register · Nov 2025
    Free walkthrough of the Nvidia, Oracle, CoreWeave, AMD and Microsoft deals.
  • Nvidia-OpenAI deal sparks concerns over circular financing in AI boom
    Bloomberg via Business Standard · Sep 2025
    Analyst reaction at announcement, including Bernstein's Stacy Rasgon.
  • SoftBank offloads entire $5.83 billion stake in Nvidia to fund OpenAI
    Tom's Hardware · Nov 2025
    The loop from another angle: SoftBank sold Nvidia shares to fund OpenAI, which buys Nvidia hardware.

8 Oracle: one notch above junk

9 Your power bill subsidizes it

Data centers are driving up wholesale electricity prices, and utilities pass those costs on to the households and businesses nearby. Part of the cost of the AI buildout ends up on ordinary power bills.
