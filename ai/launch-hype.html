## 1. Near-flagship for less (Sonnet 5) - 20 June 2026

- **Claim:** Close to Opus 4.8 performance at a lower price. ([Bleepo](https://www.bleepo.co/article/claude-sonnet-5-useless-flop-backlash-benchmarks))
- **Reality:** The measured cost per task was about $2.29, roughly 15% higher than Opus 4.8. The new tokenizer also generated about 30% more tokens for the same text. ([Spectrum AI Lab](https://spectrumailab.com/blog/claude-sonnet-5-review-2026))
- **3D scene:** A balance scale has a small box labelled "mid-tier" on one pan and a large box labelled "flagship" on the other. The camera circles the scale as small, token-shaped weights rain onto the pan holding the smaller box until it sinks below the pan holding the flagship box.

## 2. World's most advanced, for three weeks (Fable 5.1) - 1 September 2026

- **Claim:** Anthropic billed the pair as the most advanced models in the world for coding and knowledge work. ([Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-claude-fablemythos-51-new/))
- **Reality:** Opus 5.5 took first place on the Artificial Analysis index 21 days later, scoring 58 against Fable 5.1's 53. ([NYU Shanghai RITS](https://rits.shanghai.nyu.edu/ai/claude-opus-5-5-takes-the-top-spot-on-artificial-analysis-at-58/))
- **3D scene:** A golden trophy stands on a podium under a single spotlight. A wall calendar behind it rapidly flips through its pages. On the 21st flip, a second, slightly taller trophy slides into view on a cheaper-looking podium, and the spotlight swings over to it.

## 3. Cheaper workloads, bigger bills (Fable 5.1)

- **Claim:** A 75% reduction in cache-read prices, with typical workloads reported to be about 25% cheaper. ([Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-claude-fablemythos-51-new/))
- **Reality:** Output token usage rose by about 1.7x in Artificial Analysis's measurement, pushing the cost per task up by roughly 20%. ([Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/ainews-claude-fablemythos-51-new/))
- **3D scene:** A receipt printer sits on a desk and prints a short slip stamped "SAVINGS". It keeps printing. The paper spools across the desk, over the edge and across the floor as the camera pulls back and rises to reveal the room filling with receipt tape.

## 4. The vanishing lead (Opus 5.5) - 22 September 2026

- **Claim:** A Terminal-Bench 4.0 score of 66.4% put it well ahead of GPT-6 Astra's 57.9%. ([Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/claude-opus-55-challenges-gpt-6-astra-with-lower-costs-higher-scores))
- **Reality:** Artificial Analysis's rerun produced a score of 59.6%, level with Astra. ([Kingy AI](https://kingy.ai/blog/claude-opus-5-5-specs-benchmarks-pricing-comparison/))
- **3D scene:** Two glass columns fill with glowing liquid on a dark studio floor. The orange column surges far above the blue one. The camera orbits 180 degrees, the lighting shifts from warm to neutral white, and the orange liquid level drains until both columns are at the same height.

## 5. The 30% discount that cost 50% more (Sonnet 5.5) - 28 September 2026

- **Claim:** Up to 30% lower cost per task than Sonnet 5. ([Kingy AI](https://kingy.ai/blog/claude-sonnet-5-5-specs-benchmarks-pricing/))
- **Reality:** At maximum effort, the cost was about $7.60 per task in Artificial Analysis's test - roughly 50% higher than Sonnet 5 and more than Opus 5.5's $5.98. ([GlobalGPT](https://www.glbgpt.com/hub/claude-sonnet-5-5-review/), [SmartScope](https://smartscope.blog/en/blog/claude-sonnet-5-5-review-testing-smarter-than-2026/))
- **3D scene:** A "30% OFF" price tag swings from a string in front of a sleek vending machine. The camera moves past the tag towards the machine's display, where the digits roll upwards like those on a fuel pump. Coins pour in from the viewer's side of the frame and into the slot.

## 6. The seven-point haircut (Sonnet 5.5)

- **Claim:** A score of 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, beating its own flagship sibling. ([Apidog](https://apidog.com/blog/claude-sonnet-5-5-benchmarks/))
- **Reality:** The independent run scored 63.6%. Sonnet 5.5 retained its lead over Opus, but the score was lower. ([Apidog](https://apidog.com/blog/claude-sonnet-5-5-benchmarks/))
- **3D scene:** A high-jump bar is set at "70.6" in an empty stadium. A stylised figure clears it in slow motion. Cut to a second, identical stadium in flat daylight, where an official quietly lowers the bar to "63.6" and the same jump is replayed.

## 7. The ten-cent model with a cliff (Haiku 5.5) - 7 October 2026

- **Claim:** $0.10 / $0.50 per million tokens, 90% cheaper than Haiku 4.5. ([Saidul Badhon](https://saidulbadhon.com/blogs/claude-haiku-5-5-review))
- **Reality:** Every rate increases fivefold once a prompt exceeds 100k tokens. ([eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/claude-haiku-5-5-review))
- **3D scene:** A tiny paper boat glides along a calm, shallow channel marked by a small "10¢" buoy. A depth marker reading "100k" passes by. The channel floor then drops away into a waterfall. The camera tilts down to follow the boat over the edge as the buoy flips to "x5".

## 8. Beats its rival on every test (Haiku 5.5)

- **Claim:** Ahead of GPT-6 Luna on every test that both models ran. ([Saidul Badhon](https://saidulbadhon.com/blogs/claude-haiku-5-5-review))
- **Reality:** It scored 35% on AutomationBench-AA, compared with 53-60% for Luna and other rivals. At maximum effort, it also used about three times as many output tokens as Luna. ([Van Data Team](https://vandatateam.com/blog/claude-haiku-5-5))
- **3D scene:** A row of identical scoreboards lights up green, one by one, as the camera dollies sideways. The camera continues past the edge of the stage set into an unlit backstage area. There, one more scoreboard glows red, and a fuel gauge beside it drains three times faster than its neighbour.

## 9. An operating system from an empty folder (Fable 5.2) - 9 November 2026

- **Claim:** More than 1,000 subagents worked for eight days to produce a prototype operating system that boots, alongside lower token prices. ([AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-fable-5-2-builds-a-working-os-from-an-empty-folder))
- **Reality:** One tester's showcase projects incurred an estimated $14,710 in API costs and still shipped with bugs and awkward animations. ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/382897/anthropic-claude-fable-5-2-and-mythos-5-2-impressive-and-expensive))

Claude Fable 5.2 / Mythos 5.2

Launched 9 November 2026

Claimed at launch
  1. Built a prototype operating system that boots, from an empty folder, by coordinating more than 1,000 subagents over 8 days. Source: AlphaSignal · DEV Community
  2. 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours; Opus 5.5 got about a quarter of the way. Source: AlphaSignal
  3. Up to 30% faster generation and 20% lower API prices than Fable 5.1. Source: GPTunneL · CNBC launch coverage
What set in
  1. The showcase builds are expensive. One early tester's projects reached an estimated $14,710 in API costs, and still had bugs, awkward animations and untested features. Source: Decrypt
  2. The proof result covers one theorem under one scaffold, so it shows performance in that setup, not general mathematical reliability. Source: AlphaSignal
  3. Every's Dan Shipper called it "spiky": the best tool for a narrow set of long, ambitious tasks, but too slow, costly and token-hungry for routine work, and not at the frontier for writing. Hacker News commenters called it lackluster and noted the pre-IPO timing; users reported hitting usage limits within minutes. Source: BigGo Finance on Shipper's review · Hacker News · GPTunneL

Claude Haiku 5.5

Launched 7 October 2026

Claimed at launch
  1. $0.10 input / $0.50 output per million tokens: 90% cheaper than Haiku 4.5. Source: Anthropic launch page · Saidul Badhon review
  2. Beats GPT-6 Luna on every test both models were run on. Source: Saidul Badhon review
  3. Terminal-Bench 4.0 jumps from 0% (Haiku 4.5) to 39.2%. Source: Saidul Badhon review
What set in
  1. The bargain price only applies to short prompts. Past 100k prompt tokens, every rate goes up 5x. Source: eesel.ai review
  2. At max effort it used about 162,000 output tokens per task, roughly three times GPT-6 Luna. It also scored 35% on AutomationBench-AA against 53–60% for rivals. Source: Van Data Team (citing Artificial Analysis)
  3. Anthropic's own system card says it over-refused more than any other model tested. Artificial Analysis measured a 40% hallucination rate on its knowledge test. Source: AIWiz · AIToolsReview

Claude Sonnet 5.5

Launched 28 September 2026

Claimed at launch
  1. More than 30% faster than Sonnet 5 and up to 30% cheaper per task. Source: Kingy AI
  2. 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0: ahead of the flagship Opus 5.5 (66.4%) and seven times Sonnet 5's score. Source: Apidog
  3. "A sharp eye for design." Source: BigGo Finance (quoting Anthropic)
What set in
  1. The 30% saving comes from using fewer tokens on some work, not a price cut. At max effort, Artificial Analysis measured about $7.60 per task: roughly 50% above Sonnet 5, and more than Opus 5.5's $5.98. Source: GlobalGPT · SmartScope
  2. The independent Terminal-Bench rerun scored 63.6%, not 70.6% (the lead over Opus did hold). Opus 5.5 still wins seven of the eight launch tests. Source: Apidog · Emergent
  3. Theo Browne highlighted a record 271,920 tokens per task on Cursor's benchmark. The system card concedes the model is slightly more likely than Sonnet 5 to state a wrong answer, and that max effort scores lower than the setting below it on one coding test. Source: BigGo Finance on Theo Browne · ComputingForGeeks

Claude Opus 5.5

Launched 22 September 2026

Claimed at launch
  1. 66.4% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 against GPT-6 Astra's 57.9%. Source: Analytics Insight · Emergent
  2. Beats Astra on FrontierCode at roughly 20% of the cost per task, and matches it on Terminal-Bench at about 40% of the cost. Source: CellCog
  3. Generates output more than 30% faster than Opus 5. Source: CellCog
What set in
  1. Artificial Analysis's own run scored 59.6%, level with Astra rather than 8.5 points ahead. Source: Kingy AI · NYU Shanghai RITS
  2. Efficiency depends on the effort setting: at max, about 119K output tokens per task, roughly 4x Astra. Astra also leads on AutomationBench and Terminal-Bench-Science. Source: Kingy AI summary
  3. About a week in, major user threads said the model had got worse, and tracked sentiment slid from the low 70s to the mid 50s. Counterpoint: daily benchmark trackers found no confirmed regression. What is documented is that most cybersecurity tasks get rerouted to the older Opus 4.8. Source: Admix · i10x · Zoogom fact-check

Claude Fable 5.1 / Mythos 5.1

Launched 1 September 2026

Claimed at launch
  1. "The world's most advanced models for coding and knowledge work." Source: Latent Space AINews (quoting Anthropic)
  2. Terminal-Bench-Science more than doubles, from 24.7% to 52.6%. Source: Latent Space AINews
  3. Cache-read prices cut 75%, typical workloads about 25% cheaper, and about 60% fewer false-positive cyber refusals. Source: Latent Space AINews
What set in
  1. The crown lasted three weeks. Opus 5.5 then took first place on the Artificial Analysis index at 58, with Fable 5.1 at 53, for a lower price. Source: NYU Shanghai RITS
  2. Artificial Analysis measured about 1.7x more output tokens, so cost per task rose roughly 20% despite the cache discount. Source: Latent Space AINews
  3. Testers still hit false-positive safeguard blocks. Zvi Mowshowitz's read of the system card found the multi-turn bioweapons safe-response rate on the API fell from 94% to 73%. Source: Latent Space AINews · LessWrong / Zvi Mowshowitz

Claude Sonnet 5

Launched 30 June 2026

Claimed at launch
  1. Near Opus 4.8 performance at a lower price. Source: Bleepo
  2. Narrowly beat Opus 4.8 on the GDPval-AA knowledge-work test, 1,618 to 1,615 Elo. Source: Spectrum AI Lab
  3. Anthropic's most agentic Sonnet, at $2 / $10 per million tokens. Source: Spectrum AI Lab
What set in
  1. Artificial Analysis measured about $2.29 per task, roughly 15% more than Opus 4.8. Simon Willison found the new tokenizer produced about 30% more tokens for the same text. Source: Spectrum AI Lab
  2. On coding it trailed: 63.2% on SWE-bench Pro against 69.2% for Opus 4.8, and CodeRabbit measured lower bug-detection recall than Sonnet 4.6. Source: Spectrum AI Lab
  3. Fans labelled it a "useless flop" within hours. Users reported a more argumentative model with more refusals, and Sonnet 4.6 was removed from the model picker. Source: Bleepo · Windows Report · Jose Parreño Garcia
